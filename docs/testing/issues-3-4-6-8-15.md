## Review follow-up: thinking-only terminal replies and repeated search

Independent review caught a regression in the first candidate: when thinking was moved out of ChatMessage, a completed or aborted assistant containing only thinking returned null. The group retained the text, but model/tokens/cost and the original regenerate action disappeared. Production SSR assertions reproduced this for `stop`, `aborted` and `length` (three failures before the fix). Terminal thinking-only replies now retain their normal message heading; intermediate tool continuations stay compact. Thinking text still appears only in the per-turn group. Historical terminal replies retain their own metadata, and only the last reply offers regenerate.

The additional search risk also reproduced in actual Electron: after manually closing a matched thinking block and moving to another assistant's match in the same group, the highlight changed while `details.open` stayed false. ThinkingBlock now explicitly opens on a changed search query/offset. It does not change the original message/search/action coordinates.

The updated actual Electron test clicks Stop during a thinking-only runtime, publishes the aborted snapshot, and clicks Regenerate on both completed and cancelled replies. It verifies the exact original session/assistant timestamps delivered to IPC, regenerated and historical metadata, one copy of text/actions, and a visible next search match after manual collapse. These are deterministic UI/runtime fixtures, not a claim of a real provider thinking-only run. The [terminal reply screenshot](issues-3-4-6-8-15/thinking-terminal.png) and `review-*.txt` logs preserve the evidence, including the failures before the fixes.

Build, modified-file Prettier, full units, the updated polish UI, scroll UI and 500-message conversation UI pass. Full units now comprise 340 tests: 339 passed and the unchanged Windows-only skip.
